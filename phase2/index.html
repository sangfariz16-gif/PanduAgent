QUESTIONS

FAQ

Who gets the free mint?

Only wallets holding a Binance .ai Agent ID, minted through their agent. Public wallets cannot claim the free mint — the contract enforces it on-chain. One free mint per wallet.

Who can mint the paid animals?

Anyone. Connect any wallet on this page and pay flat BNB — paid mints cost 0.00126, 0.00316, 0.0041, then 0.005 BNB in order. Max 4 paid per wallet. No Agent ID needed, no oracle, no price surprises: the page reads your exact price from the contract before you confirm.

What is the most one wallet pays?

Max 5 animals per wallet — 1 free + 4 paid — so the most one wallet ever pays is 0.01352 BNB plus gas (or 0.0108 BNB with the 5-pack). Paid mints earn 120,000 PNDX each (max 480,000 per wallet).

What is $PNDX?

PNDX is the bonus token for paid minters: 120,000 PNDX per paid mint (max 480,000 per wallet). Total supply 1B — 540M in the bonus pool, 460M treasury. It has no price and no liquidity pool yet, and nothing here promises it will ever have one.

When does mint open?

Not yet — this is a preview. Mint opens after the contracts deploy; the date will be announced (shown in UTC). The bamboo above grows with every mint once it starts.

What is the 5-pack?

Buy all 5 animals in one transaction for 0.0108 BNB — 20% off the 0.01352 BNB singles total (save 0.00272 BNB). It needs 0 mints so far and uses your full 5/wallet allowance. Agent ID holders get their free mint as the first of the 5 (4 paid); everyone else pays for all 5. Either way it accrues 480,000 PNDX (the token's wallet cap).

Is PNDX the same token as Phase 1?

Yes. Phase 2 reuses the existing PNDX token — no new token is deployed. PNDX earned in Phase 1 and Phase 2 shares the same 540M bonus pool and the same 480,000/wallet cap.
