QUESTIONS

FAQ

Who gets the free mint?

Only wallets holding a Binance .ai Agent ID, minted through their agent. Public wallets cannot claim the free mint — the contract enforces it on-chain. One free mint per wallet.

Who can mint the paid animals?

Anyone. Connect any wallet on this page and pay flat BNB — paid mints cost 0.00126, 0.00316, 0.0041, then 0.005 BNB in order. Max 4 paid per wallet. No Agent ID needed, no oracle, no price surprises: the page reads your exact price from the contract before you confirm.

What is the most one wallet pays?

Max 5 animals per wallet — 1 free + 4 paid — so the most one wallet ever pays is 0.01352 BNB plus gas. Paid mints earn 120,000 PNDX each (max 480,000 per wallet).

What is $PNDX?

PNDX is the bonus token for paid minters: 120,000 PNDX per paid mint (max 480,000 per wallet). Total supply 1B — 540M in the bonus pool, 460M treasury. It has no price and no liquidity pool yet, and nothing here promises it will ever have one.

When does mint open?

Not yet — this page is a preview. The contracts deploy first; mint opens right after, and the bamboo above starts growing with the first mint.
